# Peppy product foundation

## Purpose

Peppy helps a person manage daily life with the least possible effort. It turns setup choices into gentle daily guidance, remembers routine information, and makes help easy to request.

Peppy is not a medical device, emergency service, diagnosis tool, or replacement for human care.

## Primary jobs

- Tell me the next useful thing without making me plan the whole day.
- Help me remember appointments, medication routines, food, household tasks, and personal care.
- Let me record something by talking, photographing, choosing a recent item, or typing.
- Make a hard day smaller without treating it as failure.
- Help a trusted person support me without exposing my whole life.
- Preserve my work when I am tired, interrupted, offline, or using assistive technology.

## Assistance model

Peppy starts with a guided profile chosen by the person:

- Guide me: one action at a time, larger text, fewer choices.
- Show my plan: a short ordered day with details available on demand.
- Low-energy day: only person-defined essentials and easy help access.

The profile can change at any time and is never inferred from disability or health information.

## Information architecture

1. Today — the next action, essential status, and low-energy switch.
2. Plan — routines, appointments, people, and upcoming tasks.
3. Capture — talk, photo, recent item, or type.
4. Support — trusted helpers, permissions, accessibility, privacy, and urgent help.

## Setup sequence

Setup is resumable and useful after every answer:

1. Choose assistance level.
2. Choose one area to help with first.
3. Choose reminder boundaries and quiet hours.
4. Optionally add a trusted helper.
5. Arrive at Today with one useful action.

Everything else is learned only when it becomes relevant. No complete profile is required before Peppy helps.

## Safety and dignity

- Do not infer capacity, impairment, diagnosis, mood, or risk.
- Ask before sharing, notifying, recording, or using a microphone/camera.
- Show exactly what a helper can access.
- Separate emergency information from routine reminders.
- Use neutral language: skipped, changed, or needs help—not missed, failed, or non-compliant.
- Keep a visible history of consequential changes and shared actions.

## Initial usability outcomes

A first-time user should be able to reach a useful Today screen in under 90 seconds without typing. A returning user should complete or defer the next action in two taps or fewer. Every blocked state must expose an assisted recovery path.
